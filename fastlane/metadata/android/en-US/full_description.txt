Tern is a LoRa mesh protocol that treats airtime as a shared, metered resource. This app drives a Tern node over Bluetooth LE, through Tern's companion protocol. It does nothing without a node.

Tern is alpha: the protocol is a draft, and the app changes with it.

The app:

* finds a node, pairs with it by the passkey it shows, and keeps a link to it in the background
* shows conversations with contacts and groups, where each message is and what it waits for, and notifies of new ones, with a reply from the notification
* adds contacts by QR code, link or address, each with a short code to check with its owner
* shows the node's battery, its airtime, and the nodes it hears
* changes the node's region, role, power and passkey
* updates the node's firmware over the same link, with the release ternmesh.org publishes for its board and region
* shares the user's position with the contacts and groups they choose, each at the precision they choose, and shows on a map where those sharing with them are

The phone gives the node its exact location only while the node shares with someone; the node rounds it for each destination before anything goes on the air. The map's tiles come from OpenFreeMap: the tile server sees which areas the map shows, and nothing else of the user's.

Tern is open: the specification is at github.com/ternmesh/spec, and this app's source at github.com/ternmesh/android, under the Apache License 2.0.
